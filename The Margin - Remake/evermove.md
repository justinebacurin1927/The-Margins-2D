**THE KINGDOM OF EVERMOVE**

*formerly known as Ambrosia*

A Worldbuilding Document

Current Year: 468 \| Founded: Year 0

# **H — HISTORY**

## **The Complete Timeline of Ambrosia / Evermove**

### **Year 0 — The Founding Era**

The Grayman, a nameless and tribeless figure, unites the three main tribes of the mainland. He becomes the symbol of The Axe — the silent authority above all — and the Kingdom of Ambrosia is established. The Grayman serves as The Axe I, the first ruler of Ambrosia.

The three founding tribes that formed the kingdom:

- Ashan — The Wind Tribe

- Bali — The Talon Tribe

- Bruna — The Wings Tribe

### **The Four Founding Fathers**

The Grayman was served by four representatives, each carrying a weapon symbol:

| **Weapon** | **Tribe Origin** | **Role**                    |
|------------|------------------|-----------------------------|
| The Bow    | Ashan Tribe      | Representative of the Wind  |
| The Staff  | Bruna Tribe      | Representative of the Wings |
| The Sword  | Bali Tribe       | Representative of the Talon |
| The Shield | Bali Tribe       | Representative of the Talon |

The Axe was not one of the four weapons — it was the symbol of the king alone, representing the authority above all others. The Grayman, having no tribe or name, became The Axe. Under his authority, the Four Founding Fathers were his hands and feet in laying the kingdom's foundation.

### **Year 63 — The Passing Era**

The Axe I (The Grayman) dies at the age of 90. The Bow, The Staff, and The Shield follow their liege into the afterlife. Only The Sword survives, outliving his comrades and his king.

### **Year 63 — The Sword's Reign**

The Sword becomes the 2nd King of Ambrosia, taking the title of The Axe II. He founds the Twilight Knights — a group that carries forward the will of the king. This begins the Sword-turned-Axe dynasty, where the ruling weapon symbol becomes The Axe upon coronation. Every king after the Grayman inherits the title of Axe when crowned. The weapon you carry changes when you take the throne.

Notably, the Axe II belonged to a rare long-lived human subspecies capable of living up to 200 years. He took the throne at age 92 and ruled for 87 years — dying around age 179, within his race's natural lifespan.

### **Year ~150 — The Axe III**

The Axe II passes the throne to his elder son, who becomes The Axe III — the first hereditary king of Ambrosia. As the son of a long-lived tribesman and a regular human, the human trait dominated. The Axe III inherited a normal human lifespan, effectively ending the long-lived bloodline.

### **Year ~188 — The Axe IV**

The Axe III passes the throne to his only child, who becomes The Axe IV.

### **Year ~226 — The Axe V**

The Axe IV dies after 38 years of reign. His son becomes The Axe V.

### **Year 273 — Death of the Axe V**

The Axe V dies, leaving behind five children with no clear successor. This triggers a succession crisis that will reshape the kingdom forever.

### **Year 274–281 — Clashes for the Throne**

The five children of the Axe V battle for the crown. Factions form around each candidate:

- The 1st Prince — Heavily supported by a powerful faction

- The 2nd Prince — Supported by a rival faction

- The 3rd Child — Killed during the conflict

- The 4th Child (Princess) — Eventually crowned as Queen of Ambrosia

- The 5th Child — Exiled from the kingdom

### **Year 288 — The Great Divide**

The 1st and 2nd Princes unite their factions and move to overthrow their sister, claiming she does not deserve the throne. Civil war erupts across the kingdom. The conflict lasts 6 years. The Twilight Knights, loyal to the crowned queen (the Iron Maiden), fight in her defense but ultimately fall. The Iron Maiden is killed.

### **Year 294 — The Rise of Evermove**

The Civil War ends. The 2nd Prince wins the crown. He institutes sweeping changes throughout the kingdom — and renames Ambrosia to Evermove. He is the current king as of Year 468, having reigned for 174 years through supernatural means granted by entities known as the Tribunal Beings.

### **Year 468 — Present Day**

The Kingdom of Evermove stands under the rule of its supernatural king. Most citizens alive today have no memory of Ambrosia, the Civil War, or the world before the Tribunal Beings' religion. The king's 174-year reign has normalized his authority — to the common person, he has simply always been there.

# **E — ECONOMY**

## **Currency System**

The official currency of Evermove operates on a four-tier system:

| **Currency**      | **Conversion**                        |
|-------------------|---------------------------------------|
| Copper Coin       | Base currency — smallest denomination |
| Silver Coin       | 25 Copper = 1 Silver                  |
| Gold Coin         | 10 Silver = 1 Gold                    |
| Royal Gold Plaque | 1,000 Gold = 1 Royal Gold Plaque      |

The Royal Gold Plaque is an extraordinarily rare denomination — practically mythical to common citizens. It circulates only among the highest nobility and crown institutions.

## **Wages by Occupation**

| **Occupation**         | **Monthly Wage**   | **Social Class** |
|------------------------|--------------------|------------------|
| Labor Worker           | 25 Copper / month  | Lowest class     |
| Stonecutter            | 300 Copper / month | Skilled labor    |
| Outlander Coachman     | 1 Silver / month   | Working class    |
| Lord (Territory Owner) | 20 Gold / month    | Nobility         |

The wage gap between a Labor Worker (25 copper/month) and a Lord (20 gold/month) is staggering — equivalent to 500,000 copper. This extreme inequality is a defining feature of Evermove's economic structure.

Banking infrastructure varies dramatically by region, reflecting the kingdom's inequality:

| **Region**     | **Banking Status**                                                                   |
|----------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Magnus (C)     | Multiple official banks — regulated, crown-monitored, financial heart of the kingdom |

The regions with multiple banks represent areas where the crown maintains financial visibility and control. Herois with its single bank represents the crown's loosening grip. Valens with no official banks means Duke Emery operates entirely outside the kingdom's financial oversight — a dangerous degree of independence.

## **The Tax System**

### **The Tax Chain**

Taxes flow upward through a strict hierarchy:

- Peasants pay Nobles (their direct territorial lords)

- Nobles consolidate and send Forwarding Contribute to their Duke/Marquis

- Marquises verify the amounts meet requirements before forwarding to the Capital

- The Capital (Magnus) receives the final consolidated tax

### **Forms of Payment**

Taxes can be paid in multiple forms:

- Copper, Silver, or Gold coins (preferred)

- Livestock — chickens, cows, etc. (accepted as Excess)

- Materials and goods (accepted as Excess)

- Human beings (rare, used as collateral when no other payment exists)

### **The Excess System**

Any non-monetary tax payment is classified as Excess. The Capital does not accept Excess directly — it must be converted to coin before submission. This conversion is the responsibility of the noble collecting the tax, who packages the final amount as a Forwarding Contribute.

This system creates significant corruption potential: nobles control the conversion rate of Excess to coin, meaning they can undervalue livestock, overcharge peasants, or divert human Excess through private channels (particularly into Valens' underground economy) before converting profits to coin.

### **The Crown's Policy**

The Crown and Grand Duke Orioni operate under one principle: meet the requirement. They do not audit how nobles convert Excess or how they extract taxes from their peasants. As long as the Forwarding Contribute reaches the required amount, no questions are asked.

Consequences for peasants unable to pay taxes with no collateral: imprisonment or death.

# **R — RELIGION**

## **The Three Tribal Faiths (Pre-Purge)**

### **Ashan — The Nature Faith**

The Ashan tribe believed that nature itself is God. Natural resources are blessings; disasters are divine punishment. They believed in spirits of nature inhabiting the world around them. Notably, the Ashan faith had no blasphemy laws — belief was free and open. This faith most deeply influenced the Ambrosian Imotte writing system, whose vine-like script carries the essence of nature worship into every written word.

### **Bali — The Stone Faith**

The Bali tribe carved figures into stone and believed these carvings became avatars of their gods, channeling divine strength and blessings particularly in battle. The act of carving was ritualistic — not merely artistic. Through the lens of the Mystic power system, these beliefs were literally true: collective faith accumulated in the carved stones, making them genuine vessels of power.

### **Bruna — The Star Faith**

The Bruna tribe worshipped the Night God through constellations and the night sky. Night was considered the most precious time of day — sacred rather than fearful. Many Brunens were naturally nocturnal as a cultural and spiritual practice. The Twilight Knights, founded by the Axe II, were worshippers of the Night God, carrying Bruna faith into a military institution.

## **The Grayman's Philosophy**

When the Grayman united the tribes, he layered a philosophical teaching on top of the existing faiths without outlawing them: that mastering oneself is the greatest power available to any person. He taught that moral consciousness and inner discipline were forms of strength greater than reliance on supernatural forces.

This philosophy merged with the three tribal faiths over centuries, expanding them rather than replacing them. By the time of Axe V, a unified merged faith had developed — where nature, self, night, and stone all became interconnected aspects of one worldview. It took 294 years to build. The current king destroyed it in one decree.

## **The Tribunal Beings — State Religion (Year 294–Present)**

Upon taking the throne in Year 294, the current king exterminated every existing religion — the three tribal faiths and the Grayman's philosophy alike. He announced that the true source of power lies in another dimension, inhabited by entities he calls the Tribunal Beings. He claims to have ascended through them and declared his power divine.

### **Structure of the Church**

| **Rank**     | **Description**                                                                      |
|--------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| The Pope     | Highest religious authority. Has ascended through the Tribunal Beings like the king. |
| The Saints   | Ascended individuals below the Pope. Supernaturally empowered.                       |
| Head Priests | Regional religious authority. Act as judges in legal trials.                         |
| Priests      | Local religious figures serving the temples.                                         |

Temples receive a portion of Forwarding Contributions as mandatory donations, making the Church financially powerful — essentially a second tax system feeding from the same peasant population as the crown and nobility.

The Church maintains its own military force: the Temple Retributions. This gives the Pope independent military power separate from the crown — a source of latent political tension.

### **The Heretics**

Practicing any pre-Purge religion is classified as heresy, punishable by death. Underground faithful who continue worshipping the old tribal faiths or practicing the Grayman's philosophy are known as Heretics. They are hunted by the Temple Retributions and the new Knights.

Some Heretic groups are organized networks; others are isolated individuals or families maintaining faith in secret. Region E (Herois) — being the most ignored and poorly governed region — likely harbors the highest concentration of underground faithful.

### **The Unintended Consequence**

The king purged religion to consolidate control — not because he understood the Mystic power system. By redirecting all collective belief toward the Tribunal Beings, he may have accidentally made them significantly stronger. The old Mystic symbols — stone avatars, nature spirits, star constellations — weaken from 174 years of belief starvation, while the Tribunal Beings absorb the redirected faith of an entire kingdom.

# **M — MECHANICS**

Evermove contains multiple distinct power systems that coexist and sometimes conflict. Power is not a single unified force but manifests through different philosophies and sources.

## **The Sense System — The Inner Self**

Rooted in the Grayman's philosophy of self-mastery, the Sense System is based on the five human senses. Every person has these senses, but through discipline and training, they can be awakened into extraordinary abilities. Power is earned — not gifted, not given at birth.

Notably, a blind person who awakens their Sight sense can regain vision. A deaf person who awakens Hearing gains perception beyond normal range. The Sense System offers second chances — but at a cost.

### **The Sensory Burden — Consequence System**

Overusing awakened senses triggers a three-stage consequence system:

| **Stage** | **Name**         | **Effect**                                                                                                  | **Reversibility**    |
|-----------|------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|----------------------|
| Stage 1   | Sensory Debt     | Pushing one sense causes others to weaken temporarily. Body redistributes sensory capacity.                 | Reversible with rest |
| Stage 2   | Sensory Overload | Exceeding debt threshold floods the mind with unfiltered data. Results in paralysis, seizures, or coma.     | Possible but slow    |
| Stage 3   | Echo             | Accumulated overuse leaves permanent sensory imprints. Mind cannot distinguish reality from sensory memory. | Irreversible         |

The cruel irony of the Sense System: the harder you chase mastery, the closer you get to losing the very sense you awakened. A master who pushed too far may be extraordinarily powerful but forever trapped in a fractured reality.

## **Mystic — The Collective Belief**

Mystic power is rooted in shared faith and the accumulation of collective belief over time. A single person believing something means little. Thousands believing the same thing for generations can make it literally real.

The cycle of Mystic: Belief forms around a Symbol. The Symbol accumulates the collective faith. Eventually, the Symbol manifests the power of that belief physically. The Bali tribe's stone avatars are the clearest example — centuries of collective belief made them genuine channels of divine strength.

Since the religious purge of Year 294, the old Mystic symbols have been slowly starving of belief. Underground faithful keep some symbols barely alive. Meanwhile, all redirected collective belief floods into the Tribunal Beings — making them stronger than any single Mystic symbol could ever become.

## **Magic — The Outer Self**

Where the Sense System is the Inner Self and Mystic is Collective Belief, Magic is the Outer Self — the part of a person that connects and interacts with the external world.

Magic is acquired through ritual and sacrifice. There is no mana to accumulate or channel. Instead, to control something external, you must surrender part of yourself to it:

| **Element** | **Sacrifice Required**              | **Philosophy**                                   |
|-------------|-------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------|
| Fire        | Burn your hand                      | You cannot command fire without knowing its pain |
| Wind        | Let something go permanently        | Wind holds nothing — neither can you             |
| Water       | Surrender control, go with its flow | Water never forces — it finds the path           |

The higher the sacrifice, the greater the power obtained. Being born with magic ability is extraordinarily rare — and those born with it are considered to already be part of the element rather than having merged with it.

## **Tribunal Divine Power**

The power granted by the Tribunal Beings to those who ascend through them. Currently held by the King, the Pope, and the Saints. This power enables supernatural longevity far beyond any natural limit — including beyond the 200-year lifespan of the rare long-lived human subspecies. The exact nature and limits of Tribunal Divine Power are not publicly understood.

A rare human subspecies capable of living up to 200 years once existed within the kingdom. The Axe II (The Sword) belonged to this race, which explains his 87-year reign beginning at age 92. This subspecies no longer exists within Evermove by Year 468. The current king is unaware this race ever existed. His 174-year supernatural reign, if compared to this race's natural 200-year limit, raises questions about whether his divinity is truly unique — a dangerous secret for anyone who might uncover it.

# **C — CULTURE**

## **Language — Ambrosian Imotte**

The official language of Evermove. Spoken uniformly across all regions with a consistent grammar and vocabulary. The writing system flows like interconnected vines — each letter a vine-like symbol, words connected in flowing botanical script. When read as full sentences, Ambrosian Imotte text literally resembles growing plant life.

This was a deliberate gift of the Grayman, who prioritized education and literacy across all lands. The vine-like script itself carries Ashan tribe influence — the Nature Faith is embedded in the very act of writing, even after the purge destroyed the faith itself.

### **Literacy by Class**

| **Class**                | **Literacy Level**                                                  |
|--------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Nobles, Priests, Knights | Fully literate — read and write fluently                            |
| Merchants, Craftsmen     | Mostly literate — required for trade and record-keeping             |
| Common people            | Basic understanding — can speak and recognize simple written text   |
| Peasants                 | Mostly illiterate — speak Ambrosian Imotte but cannot read or write |

### **Tribal Linguistic Traces**

Despite being a uniform language, Ambrosian Imotte carries the echo of its three founding sources:

- Ashan traces — Nature vocabulary: weather, animals, plants, seasons. Softer, more flowing pronunciation.

- Bali traces — Strength and craft vocabulary: weapons, architecture, battle terms. Harder, more percussive.

- Bruna traces — Time and sky vocabulary: constellations, navigation, calendar terms. More rhythmic.

A scholar who studies tribal etymology could reconstruct the old faiths from the language alone — making Ambrosian Imotte itself a form of living heresy that the king cannot purge without destroying the kingdom's communication infrastructure.

### **Regional Language Variation**

- Southern regions (Wallington, Mirko) — Multilingual environment due to heavy foreign trading presence. Ambrosian Imotte mixed with foreign merchant languages and pidgins.

- Herois — Foreign languages introduced by refugee communities from the western kingdom.

- Magnus and inland regions — Pure Ambrosian Imotte culture.

# **S — SOCIAL SCALE**

## **Social Mobility**

Evermove operates as a functionally closed class system. Where you are born on the social ladder is, in almost all cases, where you die.

## **The Knighthood Paradox**

A commoner who earns knighthood through years of sacrifice holds a real rank with real authority. But noble-born knights never fully accept them as equals. The rank is real. The respect is not. A commoner knight carries their origin like a shadow that follows every accomplishment.

## **The Church as Social Ladder**

The most realistic path upward for a commoner. The Church does not prioritize bloodline as much as devotion and usefulness. A talented commoner priest can theoretically rise to Head Priest — and Head Priests hold judicial power. That is remarkable power in a society this rigid. The price is complete dedication to the Tribunal Beings and the king's religious agenda.

## **Power Ability Users**

A commoner who awakens Sense abilities, practices Mystic, or attains Magic occupies an unusual social position. They can improve their lifestyle significantly and gain local reputation and influence. However, without proper training (which noble institutions provide), they rarely reach their full potential. And those nobles with access to proper training and resources will generally hold the advantage.

## **The System's Purpose**

The tax system, the religious structure, the closed social ladder — none of this is accidental or broken. It is working exactly as those in power intend. A king who has reigned 174 years has had more than enough time to ensure the ladder stays exactly where it is.

Every law reinforces it. Every tax extracts from the bottom to feed the top. Every religious decree reminds the peasant that their suffering is divinely sanctioned. The system's message to those at the bottom is simple: know your place, stay there, and be grateful you are alive.

This is the world the protagonist of Evermove inherits — born at the bottom, in the poorest region, in the town of Corneo.
