# Protagonist — Klein

Region: [[Region 1 - Forest]] (Corneo, Herois — Novelborne territory, pre-conquest)
Related: [[Lore System]] · [[Status System]] · [[Combat System]] · [[Currency System]] · [[World Structure System]] · [[Weapon System]]

## Overview
Klein is the playable character of The Margin — a young (24–26), **noble-born knight of the Kingdom of Novelborne**, freshly graduated and posted to the border town of **Corneo** on his first campaign. His assignment is quiet, ordinary, and safe — right up until Evermove invades and Herois begins to fall. The game begins as his garrison collapses around him. He starts with two things: the will to survive, and the need to understand what is actually happening. His ultimate purpose is not yet decided (see Open Items).

### The Name of the Town
**Corneo → Coneros.** Corneo is what the town is called *now*, under Novelborne. When Evermove conquers Herois, they rename it Coneros — the name it still carries fifty years later in the main story (Milek's hometown). The town's name is a casualty of the war Klein is living through.

---

## The Posting

Corneo is not a punishment and not an honor — it is a **routine first assignment** for a fresh graduate. Nobody, Klein included, expected Herois to become a war zone. It was supposed to be the safest, dullest posting on the map: a sleepy border town in the poorest, most-ignored corner of the kingdom.

He has exactly **two duties**:
1. **Guard the Copper Road** — the trade artery running through the region (ties to [[Currency System]]; copper is the base coin).
2. **Guard the town of Corneo** itself.

That is his whole world. Small, ordered, forgettable — by design.

---

## The Turn — Where the Game Begins

Then the **Evermove conquest erupts.** It blindsides everyone; a routine posting becomes a catastrophe overnight.

- His comrades begin to **desert**, are **killed**, or are **defeated by the Gilimans** (Evermove's Herois enforcers).
- The Novelborne garrison dissolves around him.
- The chain of command, the orders, the point of his two duties — all of it evaporates.

**How the game opens:** the fall is told as a **text intro** (read or skip); **gameplay begins with Klein already in the forest, fleeing the chasers** — one knight, hunted, an occupied forest, and no one left to tell him what to do. The first beat is *run and hide.*

---

## The Inversion — A Noble Made Nobody

Klein's noble birth means everything in Novelborne and **nothing in occupied Herois.** The moment Evermove owns the ground, his rank, his name, and his blood are worthless — to the Gilimans he is just one more enemy survivor to hunt. A man who has always had status is dropped, overnight, to the very bottom of a foreign kingdom's ladder (see [[Status System]] / the world's rigid class structure). The bottom of the ladder isn't where he was born — it's where the war *puts* him.

---

## Family — What the War Severs

Klein's family is safe in the **Novelborne mainland**, far from the front. The war does not take them *from* him — it takes *him from them.* An adult, quieter kind of loss: a man cut off from the life he was about to begin.

| Tie | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Magdalene** | His **fiancée**. The future the war interrupts — a marriage that hasn't happened yet. |
| **Aging parents** | Back in the mainland. The clock on their remaining years runs while he's trapped a kingdom away. |

These are the faces the survival loop carries. They are the most likely seed of his ultimate goal (*get home* is a purpose, not just "survive").

---

## Starting Drives

- **Survive** — stay alive as the garrison and the region collapse.
- **Understand** — find out what is *actually* happening. Klein is on the losing side of a war whose scale and reasons he can't see from a border town.

## Ultimate Goal — GET HOME *(LOCKED — canonical)*

Cross the conquered kingdom and get **home** to the Novelborne mainland — to **Magdalene** and his aging parents. Marry her. Reclaim the ordinary future the war stole. That's the whole of it. **The ending is locked: he makes it home** — an earned homecoming, hard-won across a lost war.

**Why it's the spine:** in a world of god-kings, Sense-users, and 174-year immortals, Klein's dream is the smallest and most human one imaginable — *survive, go home, marry the girl, live.* The ant doesn't want to kill elephants; the ant just wants to go home. That contrast — the most human goal against the most inhuman backdrop — is the heart of the game.

The two purposes *not* chosen become the first **"What if…"** branch endings in the later expansion (see [[Lore System - Storyline Roadmap]]):
- *What if he became "the ant"* — awakened Sense power at the Sensory Burden's cost (the epigraph).
- *What if he chased the buried truth* — the king's questionable divinity.

## Character & Voice (proposals — to refine)
- A man of **order and duty** dropped into a world where both are gone. His early arc may be the slow death of the belief that *doing his duty* will make the world make sense again.
- Not a hero and not a nobody — a **competent, ordinary man** in a catastrophe too big for competence. His edge is discipline and training, not destiny.
- Young enough that his whole life was still ahead of him — the career just begun, the marriage to Magdalene not yet come — which makes the loss the theft of a *future*, not a past.
