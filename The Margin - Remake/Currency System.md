# Currency System

Region: [[Region 1 - Forest]] (adapted from [[Evermove]] worldbuilding doc)
Related: [[NPCs]] · [[World Structure System]]

## Overview
Four-tier weighted currency system, sourced primarily from loot and item sell-back, spent mainly through the two trader NPCs. Currency is intentionally scarce early — Region 1 has no fixed shop, only two mobile traders.

---

## Denominations

| Currency | Conversion |
|---|---|
| Copper Coin | Base unit |
| Silver Coin | 25 Copper = 1 Silver |
| Gold Coin | 10 Silver = 1 Gold |
| Royal Gold Plaque | 1,000 Gold = 1 Royal Gold Plaque |

- Royal Gold Plaque is effectively mythical for Region 1 — not realistic loot here, exists as a world-scale detail for later regions/late-game
- Copper/Silver are the realistic day-to-day currency for Forest Region play

---

## Sources
- Tier 2/3 loot drops ([[Collapsed Watchtower]], [[Mercenary Graveyard]], [[Poacher's Camp]] stolen goods) — mostly Copper, occasional Silver
- Possibly [[The Old House]]'s locked chest
- **Sell-back:** players can sell loot to traders for coin

## Sinks
- **[[Caravan Black Market Trader]]** — primary sink, coin-only, steep/Gold-tier pricing, guaranteed presence every region
- **[[Traveling Wanderer]]** — optional sink, accepts coin or barter (Copper/Silver range)
- Future region shops (not yet built)

---

## Mechanics
- **Weight:** Coin is weighted like any other item — carrying currency costs inventory space, same as raw loot. Creates a real choice between carrying sellable loot vs. converted coin under limited capacity.
- ⚠️ Open question: does coin stack/compress (e.g., 100 Copper = 1 weight unit), or is it 1:1 like a physical item? 1:1 could get punishing fast at scale — needs a decision.

---

## Design Philosophy
- Currency is scarce by design in Region 1 — no dedicated shop, just two mobile traders
- Black Market Trader (coin-only) vs. Wanderer (flexible barter) creates a natural tier: barter = casual/early trading, coin = serious/late-game trading
- No currency sink is mandatory for survival — Wanderer's barter option means a player who never finds coin isn't hard-locked out of trading entirely; coin unlocks *better* options, not baseline access

## Worldbuilding Tie-In
