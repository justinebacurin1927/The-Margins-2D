# NPC Roster & Backgrounds

Era: ~Year 408–418 (Evermove's conquest of Novelborne-held Herois)
Related: [[NPC System]] · [[Herois Region]] · [[Protagonist - Klein]] · [[Companion System]] · [[Lore System - Storyline Roadmap]] · [[Weapon System]]

## Overview
The cast — a background for every NPC, sorted by the five roles in [[NPC System]]. **Named individuals are proposals** (rename freely); the factions, roles, and their reasons-for-being are the canon-locked part. Companion candidates are marked ⟡ and deep-dived in [[Companion System]].

---

## 🔴 Role 1 — Hostile

### The Gilimans *(faction — the occupiers)*
Herois's enforcer corps, now the spearhead of Evermove's occupation. Brutal, bureaucratic, and everywhere — they don't fight the war so much as *administer* it: patrols, curfews, sweeps, bounties on Novelborne survivors. To Klein they're the boot on the region's neck, and they **thicken every act** as the occupation tightens. The primary human threat.

- **Commander Vos** *(proposed name)* — the Giliman officer running the Coneros-district occupation. Cold, methodical, treats the hunt for stragglers as paperwork. Klein's recurring pursuer — the face of the thing chasing him home. *A named nemesis the story can escalate.*

### The Free Company *(faction — hired blades)*
Sellswords contracted from **Valens** for the conquest — in it for loot, not loyalty. This is the company whose end becomes the [[World Structure System|Watchtower → Kitchen Camp → Graveyard]] lore loop: in Klein's era they're **alive and dangerous**; by Year 468 they're bones and legend. Klein watches them earn that grave.

- **Captain [Mercenary Captain]** *(proposed — ties to the [[Weapon System|Mercenary Captain's Spear]])* — the company's leader, a hard veteran who knows the forest better than the Gilimans do. The named figure the Weapon System's T4 spear implies. His company's fate (and his own grave) is a lore thread Klein can uncover.

### Evermove Sense-Users *(the "elephants")*
The invasion's elite edge — awakened-Sense soldiers and nobles who *broke Klein's garrison in the intro.* Rare, terrifying, categorically above an ordinary knight. Mostly **scripted set-pieces or threats to avoid**, not routine fights — encountering one should read as *run, don't swing.* The living proof of the epigraph's "elephant."

- **[One named Sense-user]** *(proposed)* — a single recurring elite tied to the fall of Coneros; the personal shadow of the war for Klein. Optional antagonist for the back half.

### Predators & the Cursed *(wildlife / anomalies)*
Not political — just the forest's teeth. Wolves and coyotes (worse at night; Hunter's Blind, Watchtower), venomous snakes/insects, and the anomalous: the **Mercenary Graveyard's undead** (night-only) and the **Deep Cave Mouth guardian** (a mini-boss threshold). Danger the map itself provides.

## 🟡 Role 2 — Traders

### 🧭 The Traveling Wanderer ⟡ *(companion-adjacent)*
*A sellsword who walked away.* **Era-adjusted:** in Klein's time the Free Company is *active*, and the Wanderer is a **deserter from it** — a blade who saw where the contract was heading and left before the Graveyard claimed him. Now he trades common goods (bread, sausage, rope, coal, the odd map fragment) for **coin or barter**, drip-feeds lore about the company he abandoned, and offers side-quests. Peaceful, but an ex-merc doesn't die quietly. *(Full mechanics in [[NPC System]] Role 2.)*

### 🏴 The Caravan Black Market Trader
*Deals in what people don't ask about.* A representative of the **Black Market organization** (rooted in Valens' bankless underground), profiting off the war's chaos. **Coin only**, steeply priced, guarded by a fixed team (2 Knights, 2 Mystic-Users, 1 Magician — the game's first taste of magic). Sells debuff cures and upgraded gear; kill him and his guard and you're locked out *forever* (permanent Loss Reputation). *(Full mechanics in [[NPC System]] Role 2.)*

## 🟢 Role 3 — Neutral / World-Texture *(the companion pool)*

### ⟡ Aldric *(proposed)* — the Fellow Knight *(deserter/survivor)*
A Novelborne soldier who survived the fall of Coneros beside Klein — the one comrade who didn't desert or die. Shaken, loyal, competent with a blade. The **first companion**: a brother-in-arms who wants the same thing Klein does — to get out alive.

**Opening role (locked):** Klein flees the fall **with Aldric at his side**, and Aldric is the **diegetic tutorial** — he teaches the player how to play (controls, hiding, the survival rules) during the escape. **Then he's captured** the moment the how-to-play completes, tearing the companion away and planting a **rescue-Aldric quest** for later. He is the game's first companion *and* its first loss — *the man who taught you to survive is the first thing the war takes.*

### ⟡ Mara *(proposed)* — the Refugee Mother *(refugee)*
A displaced Coneros townswoman shepherding what's left of a family through the forest. Not a fighter — but she knows the town's hidden stores, the safe hollows, who owed whom. Trades small, gives shelter-and-supply quests, and puts a *human cost* on the war Klein's living. A companion of a different kind: not a sword, but a reason.

### ⟡ Old Fen *(proposed)* — the Hermit *(hermit)*
A forest-dweller who was old before the war and will be here after it. Knows Herois like his own hands — foraging, safe routes, weather, which mushrooms kill. A **survival mentor**: the NPC who teaches (diegetically) the systems that keep Klein alive. Wary of all sides; loyal to the woods.

### ⟡ Sister Yenna *(proposed)* — the Heretic *(underground faithful)*
An underground keeper of a pre-Purge faith (Ashan nature-worship or Bruna star-faith), hunted long before Evermove arrived. Holds the **deepest lore** — the old religions, the Mystic, the whisper of the Sense — and is the single thread toward the "Ant" What-if branch. Trusts no one quickly; what she knows could change what Klein *is.*

### Passing Neutrals *(unnamed texture)*
Other survivors, scavengers, a frightened poacher, a dying deserter with a last message — one-scene NPCs who make the forest feel *inhabited* and seed discovery-quests. No backgrounds needed; they're the crowd the named cast stands out from.

## 🔵 Role 4 — Quest-givers *(overlap)*

Quest-givers aren't a separate cast — they're the NPCs above, wearing a second hat, plus **no-giver discoveries** (journals, grave markers, a mercenary's letter). The **act-gating main quests** (the escalation trigger, see [[Lore System - Storyline Roadmap]]) most likely run through **Aldric** (the road home), a **story discovery** (the war's shape), and **Sister Yenna / the Wanderer** (the underground and the company). Side-quests come from Mara, Old Fen, and the traders.
