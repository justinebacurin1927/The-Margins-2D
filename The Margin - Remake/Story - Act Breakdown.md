# Story — Act Breakdown

Related: [[Lore System - Storyline Roadmap]] · [[Prologue - The Fall of Corneo]] · [[Protagonist - Klein]] · [[Herois Region]] · [[NPC Roster]] · [[Companion System]] · [[Quest System]] · [[Gameplay Roadmap]]

## Overview
The detailed expansion of the acts framed in [[Lore System - Storyline Roadmap]]. Each act gets: **Situation · Where · Cast · Beats · Act-gating quest · Systems · Emotional arc · Revelations.** Specific quests and scenes are **proposals** — the act *shapes*, drivers, and cast roles are locked; the exact beats are clay. Danger rises **west→east** (see [[Herois Region]]); the occupation tightens each act.

**The spine, one line:** *A knight loses the war on day one, learns to survive, learns what's really happening, and fights home to marry the girl — losing and (maybe) reclaiming the friend who taught him to live.*

## Act 0 — The Last Ordinary Day *(text intro + tutorial)*

- **Delivery:** the [[Prologue - The Fall of Corneo|prologue]] text (read/skip) → gameplay opens with **Klein and Aldric fleeing** into the pines.
- **Tutorial:** **Aldric teaches the how-to-play** diegetically on the run — move, hide, read danger, the survival basics.
- **Gate:** clear the how-to-play → **Aldric is captured**; Klein escapes alone. Seeds the **rescue-Aldric** thread.
- **Systems:** movement, hiding/detection, survival intro. **Emotional:** camaraderie → sudden, total loss.

## Act 1 — Ash and Pine *(Survive)*

**Situation:** Alone and hunted, Klein has to become a survivor — in the western border woods, the safest edge of a forest that still wants him dead.

**Where:** the **western border belt** + near woods. Tier 1 locations (Hunter's Blind, Fallen Log Hollow, Forest Shrine, Beehive Grove) + the first **Kitchen Camp**.

**Cast (in order):** grief and the empty forest → **Old Fen** (the hermit; survival mentor) → first **Giliman patrol** (evade) → first **Wanderer** trade → glimpse of **refugees** (Mara's group).

**Beats:**
1. **The first solo night** — Aldric gone, the survival loop fully on. Klein at his lowest and least competent.
2. **Old Fen** — met by sign, then in person; he teaches the deeper forest (forage, cook with Coal, purify water, read weather). The diegetic systems-mentor *(proposal: he helps because the woods hate the Gilimans more than they hate a stray Novelborne)*.
3. **First patrol** — the Gilimans are *settling in*, not passing through. Hide, don't fight. The occupation is real.
4. **First trade** — the **Wanderer** (Free Company deserter); the forest has its own economy and factions.
5. **The human cost** — stumble on **refugees** (Mara's family); the war has victims who aren't soldiers.
6. **A thread pulls east** — signs of the **Free Company** in the woods, and word that **Aldric was taken *east*, along the Copper Road,** toward the occupiers' hold.

**Act-gating quest *(proposal)*:** *"Follow the Road."* To learn Aldric's fate and the war's shape, Klein must reach the **Copper Road corridor** (a Tier-2 push). Arriving there ends Act 1 — survival alone is no longer enough; he needs to *understand*.

**Systems:** the full survival suite (Hunger/Thirst/Temperature/Weather/Debuff/Food), foraging, cooking, detection/hide.
**Emotional:** grief → grim competence; **noble made nobody.** Klein stops being a knight who lost a battle and becomes a man who intends to see tomorrow.
**Revelations:** the occupation is permanent; the Free Company exists; **Aldric is alive, taken east.**

## Act 2 — What the Fog Hides *(Understand)*

**Situation:** Klein pieces together the war and the forest's factions, gathers allies, and hunts for a way to reach Aldric — as the occupation closes in.

**Where:** the **central Copper Road corridor.** Tier 2 (Collapsed Watchtower — canon, on the Herois–Magnus border; Poacher's Camp; Sunken Well) + deeper Kitchen Camp. Danger climbs.

**Cast:** **Commander Vos** (the Giliman who takes Klein's hunt *personally* — introduced as the recurring nemesis) · the **Free Company + its Captain** (alive, dangerous) · **Mara** (recruitable) · **Sister Yenna** (first contact — the underground) · scattered **deserters/survivors**.

**Beats:**
1. **The Road** — the occupiers' artery laid bare: patrols, the Watchtower, the war's logistics. The scale lands: this is annexation, not a raid.
2. **Recruit Mara** *(quest: get her family to a safe holdout)* → non-combat companion; supply sense; the war's human cost made personal.
3. **The Free Company's arc** — meet the mercenaries; learn they're hired Valens blades; watch the **Watchtower → Kitchen Camp → Graveyard** loop begin to close (their end is happening *now*).
4. **The underground** — **Sister Yenna** and the Heretics; the old faiths, the **Mystic/Sense** truth, and the first distant thread of the **buried truth** (the king's questionable divinity). She trusts slowly.
5. **Locate Aldric** — piece together where he's held (a Giliman camp, further east); the **rescue plan** forms.
6. **Vos closes in** — the personal hunter; the occupation tightens (the story ramp made flesh).

**Act-gating quest *(proposal)*:** *"The Rescue"* — reach and attempt Aldric's prison. **Success** (Aldric rejoins) or **failure** (lost, deeper) both deliver the same turn: Klein now *knows* the war, and knowing forces a choice. → Act 3.

**Systems:** NPC factions & disposition, companions (recruit Mara, begin Yenna), trade/economy, deeper combat, the lore/underground, VOICE/dialogue.
**Emotional:** knowledge as burden; the human cost; **fight-vs-flee** (Aldric's ghost, then his rescue-or-loss).
**Revelations:** the conquest's true scale; the Free Company's fate; the old faiths & Sense; the buried-truth fuse; **Aldric found.**

## Act 3 — The Weight of a Name *(Decide → GET HOME, canonical)*

**Situation:** Klein commits. Everything points **west, to the border, to home** — through an occupation that has become a closing trap.

**Where:** a last venture **east/interior** for what the crossing demands — Tier 3 (**The Old House**, the **Mercenary Graveyard**; the **Deep Cave Mouth** as a threshold to *avoid*, not the exit) — then the hard drive **west to the NW border crossing.**

**Cast:** **Old Fen** (guide to the crossing) · **Yenna** (if bonded — the "Ant" seed) · **Aldric** (if rescued — rejoins, fight-vs-flee resolved between them) · **Vos** (final pursuit) · the **Free Company's grave** (lore payoff).

**Beats:**
1. **The choice — home.** Not glory, not vengeance. He wants Magdalene, his parents, the ordinary life. The ant just wants to go home.
2. **Last provisioning** — the Tier 3 reach: the **Old House** (a civilian family's tragedy — the war's other victims) and the **Mercenary Graveyard** (the Free Company's end, the loop closed; rare gear). Avoid the cave.
3. **The Aldric resolution** — rescued, he stands at the crossing with Klein; lost, his absence drives it. Either way it defines the final push.
4. **The closing trap** — the occupation at its worst (harsh season, systematic sweeps); **Vos's** last pursuit.
5. **The crossing** — the NW border gauntlet: the way home. *(Proposal: a final tense run, not a boss — the survival game's climax is escape, not a duel.)*
6. **The ending (LOCKED):** **he makes it home.** The reunion (mainland, Magdalene, marriage) plays out in the **Epilogue**, beyond the playable region.

**Act-gating:** the **border crossing = the win.**
**Systems:** everything at max pressure; the escalated world-state.
**Emotional:** the smallest human goal against the most inhuman backdrop — an **earned** homecoming.
**What-if seeds (later expansion):** **Yenna's bond → the Ant** (awaken Sense); **chase the buried truth** — the two un-chosen purposes become the first branch endings.

## Epilogue — Legacy *(ties to Year 468)*

Klein's personal ending is hopeful; the region's is not — and that contrast is the point.
- **Corneo becomes Coneros** — the renamed, occupied town Milek is born in, 50 years on.
- **The Mercenary Graveyard** — the main story's "ancient lore" is the grave Klein watched dug.
- **The occupation, the Gilimans, the poverty and fog** of Milek's Herois all *begin here.*
- **The underground** Klein touched (Yenna, the Heretics) is the hidden faithful still alive in 468.
- **Klein goes home** — one man escapes the margins, even as the margins stay broken.

## The Rescue-Aldric Thread *(spanning)*
Because it crosses acts, tracked here: **Act 0** he's taken → **Act 1** learn he's held east → **Act 2** locate + attempt → resolves in **Act 2 climax or Act 3**. Pulls Klein *east* (danger) against home's *west* pull — the central tension of the map made personal.
