- (direction by user) Structure the game as Region(arc)-by-Region like SPD's 5-floors-per-region, but explicitly NOT deepening-to-deepening (horizontal caravan road, not vertical descent)
- (idea by user) Food/hunger system with states: Well fed (20) -> Stable (50) -> Hungry (50) -> Starving (15)
- (idea by user) Hidden environmental items in Forest region: Tall Grass->Corn,Berries | Logs->Mushroom,dried moss,Tinder Bundle | Tree->Wet&Dry Branches,bark | Rock->Pebbles
- (technique) started Steal & Twist (mine survival/roguelikes, twist off their axis)
- (insight by coach) Removing descent removes SPD's built-in clock; food/hunger becomes the new clock that forbids camping and forces forward motion. Regions = different flavors of scarcity (Forest feeds you, next region starves you), so what you carry OUT is the real decision. Loop = provisioning across a map that gets hungrier, not descending toward a boss.
- (insight by coach) STORY SPINE: The Margins is a 31-chapter dark-fantasy novel (war, political intrigue, coming-of-age). Overall arc order (from Arcs.md): Pre-7-years -> Academic -> Revolution -> Throne War -> One Kingdom -> The Heaven Fell. Core: brothers/friends Milek (strategist) & Galleon (Alpha Wolf) separated by slave trade, fighting to reunite across the continent of Evermove. It is a horizontal ROAD story of separation+reunion -> validates 'region-by-region, NOT deepening-to-deepening'.
- (insight by coach) BIG ONE: The Forest region ALREADY EXISTS as Chapter 3. It literally contains the survival+stealth+identify loop the user is inventing: 8yo Milek & 6yo Erik flee to the north pines, shelter in a hollow rotted pine trunk lined with moss, eat forest forage (bitter herbs, mushrooms Milek checks 3x = identify-on-use poison risk, berries 'fairly certain weren't poisonous'), build a waxy-leaf+bark water collector, need FIRE + WATERPROOF. Hunger ('it was not enough / I'm still hungry') is what FORCES Milek to raid a Lamilla military camp over 5 nights (stealth: learns which soldiers sleep lightly). The forage list maps 1:1 to user's Tall Grass/Logs/Tree/Rock table.
- (idea by coach) MECHANIC from Ch3 climax: on the 5th raid Milek goes DEEPER for a waxed canvas (higher risk) and gets FOLLOWED back to the trunk by two knights -> straight to sleeping Erik. So getting detected shouldn't just raise an alarm; it should let enemies TRAIL you home and endanger your companion/hideout. Stealth failure = a threat to what you're protecting, not just to you.
- (insight by coach) THE ROAD IS ALREADY MAPPED: Swan's slave-smuggling route = your caravan road, horizontal & region-by-region: Coneros -> North Pines(forest) -> Pinehurst(lawful town) -> Tradewick(Wallington gray-law market) -> Coastal Road -> Oakdale -> Mirko -> unmapped Mountain Pass -> Valens(prison). Non-descending, coming-of-age journey. The MVP 'Route 1 slice' = the North Pines forest leg (Ch3).
- (technique) started First Principles (what is each system FOR in the player's head?)
- (idea by coach) REGION = a leg of a fixed itinerary, not a dungeon floor. The convoy/schedule moves whether you're ready or not -> the clock is horizontal (the road advances) + hunger, never descent. Progression pressure = 'the caravan leaves at dawn', not 'go deeper'.
- (idea by coach) Regions differ in KIND, not depth: Forest=forage-rich/predator-sparse; Pinehurst=lawful (you CAN'T act, watched); Tradewick=gray-law (anything permitted, bodies are the market); Coastal=exposure/salt-cold; Mirko=altitude scarcity; Valens=prison starvation. Each region's identity = 'what scarcity rule + what security layer'.
- (idea by coach) Security layer re-tunes stealth per region: knights escort to Herois border then LEAVE -> local mercenaries hired per territory -> gray-law town has no rules -> mountain bandits. 'The opening' appears in the HANDOFF between escorts. Stealth ruleset is regional, not global.
- (idea by coach) THEMATIC PILLAR = the title. 'The Margins': you operate in the slack a system leaves — the two-finger gap in the caravan door, the furthest seat from the window, the minutes between one escort leaving and the next arriving. Every region's design question: 'what margin does this place leave you?'
- (idea by coach) Hunger is 'information, not emergency' (Milek's forest lesson) -> UI frames the hunger meter as a resource you READ and spend risk against, not a panic bar. Well-fed = patience affordable; Starving = forced into raids you'd never risk fed.
- (technique) started Morphological Mixing (cross forage x need, region x security, food x bond)
- (idea by coach) Forage nodes = environmental loot from Ch3, two output tracks: FOOD (hunger) vs CAMP-SUPPLY (survive the night). Tall Grass->corn,berries(food); Logs->mushroom(food/risk),moss(warmth),tinder(fire); Tree->wet&dry branches(fire),bark(water collector); Rock->pebbles(tool). Foraging is the peaceful loop between stealth beats.
- (idea by coach) Wet vs Dry branch = a MOISTURE state that couples weather to survival: dry burns, wet won't; rain soaks your thin blanket (Ch3: 'the blanket will get wet if it rains'). Weather attacks the camp-supply track, forcing the waterproofing sub-goal (the stolen waxed canvas).
- (idea by coach) Identify-on-use grounded: the mushroom check. Milek 'checks three times before letting Erik touch'. So identify = a TIME/turn cost + a VOICE/Instinct check; eat unidentified = gamble (feed or poison). Feeding a poison mushroom to a companion is the horror version of the mechanic.
- (idea by coach) Crafting recipes are literally Ch3's to-do list: moss=bedding/warmth; tinder+dry branch=fire; bark+waxy leaves=water collector; canvas=waterproofing. The chapter is a crafting tutorial already written — 'we still need fire / and something waterproof'.
- (idea by coach) Pebbles bridge survival into your EXISTING Epic 2 systems: throw a pebble to make Noise elsewhere and pull a patrol off its route (Detection/Noise propagation already shipped). Forage feeds stealth directly — the survival and stealth systems share a currency.
- (idea by coach) The CAMP RAID loop (Ch3): sneak an enemy camp over multiple nights, learn which guards sleep lightly, take food+tools. Escalating greed: each deeper night yields better loot (bread->rope->blanket->eggs->waxed canvas) but raises risk. Night 5 (going deepest for canvas) is when it breaks.
- (idea by coach) BEST STEALTH TWIST — detection = getting TRAILED HOME. Being spotted doesn't just raise an alarm; a patient enemy FOLLOWS you back to your shelter and sleeping companion (the two knights trail Milek to the hollow trunk). Failure threatens what you're protecting. A 'tail' you must lose before returning to camp.
- (idea by coach) THE FEEDING LINE as a caged-region beat: when captured (Pinehurst/Tradewick/prison legs), you don't forage — food arrives on a schedule you don't control, and POSITION IN LINE decides who eats. Two kids in Caravan 004 DIED from being 'too far from the windows'. Starvation is lethal and systemic, not just personal.
- (idea by coach) 'The gap in the door' OBSERVATION mechanic: even caged, you play by WATCHING — count guards, time patrols, read the tone of voices ('read weather by pressure not cloud'). Intel accumulates into a hidden 'opening' meter; enough intel unlocks an escape action. Agency without movement.
- (idea by coach) SPEARSHOT = the unwinnable enemy you must NOT fight. He 'intercepts the punch before it's committed to'. A threat that punishes ANY aggression instantly; the only counter is avoidance/invisibility. Teaches the player that combat is a trap and stealth is survival — a walking do-not-engage.
- (technique) started Assumption Reversal (flip a load-bearing assumption)
- (idea by coach) Reverse 'you escape the convoy': the smartest play is often to STAY cooperative and ride the road (Milek: 'We'll come with you. No resistance.'). The convoy is the safest path deeper toward reunion; you bank intel and pick the ONE real opening, not flee the first exit.
- (idea by coach) Reverse 'hoard food': in a starvation game, GIVING food is the winning move — Henry gives Milek bread and earns loyalty; sharing builds the bond/party that later saves you. Generosity as strategy; the meter you spend buys people, not just calories.
- (idea by coach) Reverse 'kill the boss to win': victory vs Spearshot/guards is being IGNORED — 'Hide. Don't make yourselves interesting.' Stealth = SOCIAL invisibility (don't be worth noticing), not just breaking line-of-sight. A 'notability/interest' stat separate from visual detection.
- (idea by coach) Reverse 'death is the fail state': the real loss is SEPARATION/being SOLD. Losing a companion to the auction (Henry taken; Milek & Galleon split to different caravans) hurts more than dying. Fail states are about who you lose, not whether you live.
- (idea by coach) Reverse 'you choose the path': the MERCHANT chooses the route; your agency lives entirely in the margins of a fixed itinerary. That IS 'The Margins' as a design pillar — freedom is what you do in the slack, not where you go.
- (technique) started Companion/Bond mixing (ties to Epic 4 Galleon)
- (idea by coach) Galleon's loyalty is a LIABILITY mechanic: his emotion overrides your plan — he throws the punch that 'costs the opening'. A companion with a self-directed impulse meter that can trigger on provocation (a child hurt nearby), blowing your stealth. Managing his temper IS the gameplay.
- (idea by coach) A PARTY THAT GROWS is dual-edged: each person accumulated (Henry, Pack) adds a capability but also a mouth to feed (hunger) and a body to hide (bigger detection surface). 'Accumulation in a situation designed to make accumulation impossible' — party size is a risk dial.
- (idea by coach) Companions as passive INTEL sources: assign roles (Rooster counts guards, Perfect watches how mercenaries move). Each companion auto-feeds the 'opening' meter with a different data type — reason to keep/protect them beyond combat.
- (idea by coach) Companion TEMPERAMENTS change night utility: Charcoal 'sleeps when scared', Milek 'watches the door'. A watch-rotation system where who guards the camp at night depends on temperament + current hunger/fear state.
- (idea by coach) SEPARATION as a structural region-event: the road physically splits your party (different caravans; Milek sold to Yuna while Galleon rides on to Mirko). A region can strip a companion away for arcs at a time; reunion is the long-game goal that survives permadeath's sting.
- (insight by coach) SYNTHESIS SPINE: The Margins = a horizontal survival-stealth roguelike where hunger (not descent) is the clock, forage & feeding-lines are the resource loop, detection means being trailed home to those you protect, the merchant fixes the route while you work the margins, and the emotional engine is holding a growing party together against forced separation. Forest leg (Ch3) is the fully-authored MVP vignette.
