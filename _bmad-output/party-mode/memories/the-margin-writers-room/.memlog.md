# Party Memory — The Margin Writers' Room

Party: the-margin-writers-room (open-cast, conjured for narrative design of "The Margin")
Topic: the ultimate goal / driving purpose for the protagonist, and the game's introduction.

## Cast (walk-ons — not in installed roster)
- 🖋️ Reyes — Narrative Designer. Character-first; wound/want/lie; allergic to gameplay-goals-as-story.
- ⚙️ Oko — Roguelike Systems Designer. If the systems can't express it, it's set dressing. Verbs and loops.
- 📜 Maester Vell — Worldbuilding Lore-keeper. Guards Evermove canon; drops lore anvils; pedantic, protective.
- 🧠 Dr. Sabine — Player Psychology. One question: why does the player press turn 400?

## Locked before the party (project decisions)
- Universe: same as The Margins. Region = Herois. Era ~Year 408–418 (50–60 yrs before main story, Year 468).
- Placement: the LAST DAYS of Novelborne-held Herois. Herois is Novelborne territory now; Evermove conquers it (canon: it's Evermove's poorest Region E by 468).
- Protagonist: a Novelborne refugee/orphan living in Herois. The conquest ORPHANS him on-screen.
- Structure: one continuous life (permadeath = the end, SPD-style), NO floor-descent. Survive ACROSS a persistent Herois, not down it.
- Ultimate goal/spine: STILL OPEN — Justine wants a real purpose beyond survival.

## Party takeaways
- "Survive" is not a goal (Sabine) — it's the absence of failure. Need a thing moved TOWARD.
- Candidate goals surfaced & fought over:
  - Escape to Novelborne → DEMOTED from goal to the MAP itself (Oko conceded).
  - Reunion — a person on the far side of the forest worth crossing it for (Reyes).
  - Become "the ant that kills elephants" via the Sense System; the Sensory Burden (permanent blindness/deafness/madness from overuse) is a permadeath-flavored cost (Vell, off Justine's own epigraph).
  - Sabine's KNOT: want = a person; method = the ant (Sense power); cost = your senses/humanity. "Powerful FOR someone."
- Saga-level fuse (Vell): the king's 174-yr "divinity" may be a LIE — a long-lived subspecies (up to 200 yrs) he may not know existed. A buried-truth thread with a fuse.

## Introduction design (strong consensus)
- The intro = the HOUR Herois's border changes hands. The conquest makes the orphan on-screen. (Never written in canon before.)
- Play a "before": an ordinary bottom-class Novelborne morning = the TUTORIAL. Fetch water→Thirst; eat→Hunger; a coat→Temperature. Then the FALL reuses those verbs in fear.
- The fall must feature Evermove Sense-users (the elephant in the flesh); the Novelborne Lamilla try to hold and FAIL; Gilimans arrive as occupiers. Last image of the old world: a Novelborne knight who couldn't save it.
- The WOUND born in the intro = the seed of the ultimate goal.
