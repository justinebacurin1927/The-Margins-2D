- (decision) Map structure: ONE CONTINUOUS TILED Herois region (fixed canon landmarks + procedural wilderness between), not connected sub-areas. Justified: the east/west danger gradient and the spatial spine (home west, invasion east) must be felt as a single traversable landscape; the foray loop (leave safe point -> travel -> scavenge -> return) needs continuous travel; stitching sub-areas would fragment the persistent-Herois premise. PRD §8/Q2 recommends HYBRID; this resolves its continuous-vs-sub-areas fork toward continuous. Prevents: a tilemap that can't express the gradient, and a sub-area map that breaks the 'one life across a persistent Herois' promise.
- (decision) Companion model: FULL TILE-AGENT with a behavior state machine (FR-15/16). Current Companion.followStep is a greedy follower; upgrade to: own Status block, own HP, own condition/debuff state, and an AI state machine (FOLLOW / HOLD / HIDE / DISTRACT / FIGHT / RETREAT / FLEE) driven by the same detection/noise rules as enemies, replacing followStep as the primary behavior. Combatants vs non-combatants differ by behavior set, not entity depth. Prevents: a companion that is 'just following the player', which fails FR-15/16 and the help-and-liability design.
- (decision) Act-gating: main-story quests gate Act 1->2->3 via the Dialog/Quest systems (FR-18). The two PRD proposals lock: 'Follow the Road' (reach the Copper Road corridor) gates 1->2, 'The Rescue' (reach/attempt Aldric's prison; success or failure both turn) gates 2->3. Quest completion flips run-scoped flags (AD-7 FlagStore) that the occupation-escalation ramp reads. Prevents: act boundaries leaking into mechanics, and act-gating being implemented as a hard floor-check.
- (decision) Border-crossing win (FR-18): the win is REACHING the NW border tile and surviving a final tense run — not a boss fight. The border crossing is the climax; Deep Cave Mouth is a separate Region-2 threshold, NOT the exit. Mechanically: a scripted escape-run sequence over a bounded set of turns, ending in the crossing. Prevents: a boss-duel climax that contradicts the 'escape, not a duel' design and the small-human-goal thesis.
- (decision) Survival-clock honesty (FR-4): a turn is only committed when the player actually acts (moves, attacks, uses, waits). A keypress into a wall or inert tile spends no turn and ticks no survival clock. This is the existing TurnEngine behavior (acted=true only on real actions). Ratified. Prevents: survival meters draining on wasted keypresses, breaking the 'survival is choice, not chores' design.
