normalization.

Create one complete original multi-tile Hunter's Blind for The Margins 2D, a dark medieval forest
survival roguelike. It is an explorable raised wooden hunting platform, not a one-tile landmark
icon. Show a broad weathered plank deck, heavy posts and cross-braces, a short ladder/ramp entering

north lookout rail, coiled rope, compact tool crate, tracking scratches, and one cracked weak-plank
patch. Add an irregular apron of roots, leaf litter, low ferns, and moss around the outside while
keeping the south approach clear. Use crisp deliberately low-resolution top-down orthographic pixel
art, chunky clusters, hard edges, and a pine-green, bark-brown, wet-gray, muted-amber palette.
Center the complete square structure on one perfectly flat solid #ff00ff chroma background.
No characters, animals, text, UI, grid, roof, cast shadow, isometric perspective, watermark,
smooth painting, or copied game art. Do not use #ff00ff inside the structure.
