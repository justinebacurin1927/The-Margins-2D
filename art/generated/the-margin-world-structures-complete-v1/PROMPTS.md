# Remaining World Structures — final prompt set

Mode for every image: built-in image generation, followed by local magenta chroma removal and
nearest-neighbour normalization to a 9×9 atlas.

All prompts shared this production tail:

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: production source for a multi-tile top-down pixel-art structure in a 24px-grid dark
medieval forest survival roguelike. Make one complete explorable location, not a one-tile icon.
Isolate it on one perfectly uniform flat solid #ff00ff chroma background with generous padding.
Use crisp deliberately low-resolution top-down orthographic pixel art, chunky color clusters,
hard pixel edges, no antialiasing, and a moody pine-green, bark-brown, wet-gray, muted-amber
palette. Match the visual density and scale of the existing Hunter's Blind and Old House. Center
one complete square structure intended for an exact 9×9 atlas; put a clear entrance at the exact
south center and keep a visible walkable central route. Add a one-cell-looking forest transition
apron. No characters, text, UI, grid, watermark, cast shadow, isometric perspective, smooth
painting, copied artwork, or #ff00ff inside the structure. The outside chroma must contain no
gradient, shadow, texture, or stray pixels.
```

## Fallen Log Hollow

```text
An enormous ancient moss-covered fallen tree trunk spans the north and center. Split it open into
a broad readable hollow chamber with an earthen interior and south opening. Add broken bark ribs,
roots, shelf mushrooms, hanging moss, leaf bedding, edge mushroom clusters, and one abandoned
satchel. Keep the route into and through the hollow unobstructed.
```

## Forest Shrine

```text
An ancient moss-covered stone shrine: cracked south flagstone path, raised three-stone north altar,
short ruined walls, two carved standing stones with non-letter spiral and leaf motifs, offering
bowls with bread and cheese, dead candle, amber flowers, roots, ferns, and wet stones. Keep the
central ritual court open; walls, altar, and standing stones occupy clear blocking cells. No magic
glow or readable runes.
```

## Beehive Grove

```text
A grove ringed by six thick old trees and interlocking roots, with several golden-brown hives on
branches and trunk hollows. Add restrained bee specks, honeycomb pieces, a smoke pot, amber flowers,
ferns, and moss. The trees form blocking edge cells around a central harvest clearing and exact
south path. Do not cover the playable center with canopy; no giant bees.
```

## Worn Down Kitchen Camp

```text
A roofless abandoned mercenary field kitchen with torn canvas cooking shelter on the north edge,
cold stone cookfire and iron pot, preparation table, stools, firewood, barrels, sacks, drying rack,
utensils, ale jug, and spoiled remnants. Keep the muddy south path and broad working aisle clear;
place bulky stations around the perimeter. No characters, corpses, fresh meat, or active flame.
```

## Collapsed Watchtower

```text
The roofless base and collapsed lower levels of a mercenary watchtower: broken wet-stone
foundation, timber platform, surviving north/east wall, outward-fallen west beams and masonry,
snapped ladder, tilted stair, rusted spear, worn knife, unmarked torn cloth, rope, and edge rubble.
Preserve a stone-and-plank south route to the central floor. Strong asymmetric collapsed silhouette;
no intact tall tower extending out of frame.
```

## Poacher's Camp

```text
A concealed active forest camp with two low patched hide tents at the north-west and north-east,
cold firepit, small-game drying rack, skinning table, chest, cages, rope, snares, and one sprung bear
trap off the main route. Camouflage branch screens ring the sides; a south muddy trail branches
through the open center. No characters, corpses, gore, humanoid remains, or active flames.
```

## Sunken Well

```text
An old circular stone well sunk into a collapsed mossy courtyard. The broken north-center ring
shows dark fresh water; a snapped winch and frayed rope lean across it. Add descending flagstones,
cracked retaining walls, wet channels, rubble, reeds, ferns, mushrooms, roots, subtle coin glints,
and a lost pouch. A safe south flagstone route reaches the near rim; side collapses and open water
are clearly blocking. No creatures, tentacles, or magic glow.
```
